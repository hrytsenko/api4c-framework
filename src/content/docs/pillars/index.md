Pillars for **developer productivity** reduce delays for teams that design and integrate APIs:

- **[Circulation](/api4c-framework/pillars/circulation/)** aims for the discoverability of contracts through specifications.
- **[Consistency](/api4c-framework/pillars/consistency/)** aims for the replicability of decisions through guides.

Pillars for **customer satisfaction** reduce disruption for customers when APIs change:

- **[Continuity](/api4c-framework/pillars/continuity/)** aims for the compatibility of changes through policies.
- **[Conformance](/api4c-framework/pillars/conformance/)** aims for the correctness of behaviors through tests.
