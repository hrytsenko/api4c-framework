> _A man with a watch knows what time it is. A man with two watches is never sure._
>
> — Segal's law

Circulation aims for the discoverability of contracts. It relies on specifications to define contracts, registries to serve specifications to machines, and portals to expose contracts to people.

## Concerns

- **Tribal knowledge** occurs when teams neglect to describe contracts in specifications. This delays integration due to contracts that exist only in code and in people's heads.
- **Split brain** occurs when teams neglect to keep every version of a specification in one authoritative place. This delays development due to pipelines and runtimes that consume conflicting or outdated copies.
- **Hidden contracts** occur when teams neglect to expose contracts to people outside the owning team. This increases duplication due to teams rebuilding APIs they cannot find.

## Actions

- Publish **specifications** to describe contracts.
- Adopt **registries** to serve every version of a specification to machines from one authoritative place.
- Adopt **portals** to expose contracts to people, alongside the services and teams that own them.
